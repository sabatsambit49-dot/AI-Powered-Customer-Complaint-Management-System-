Event & Quality Complaint Details:
Patient (42-year-old male) was prescribed Amoxicillin/Clavulanate Batch AMX-9903 for sinusitis. Within 45 minutes of taking the first tablet, the patient developed acute urticaria, facial swelling, and severe gastrointestinal cramping. Physician notes suspect chemical contamination or active ingredient degradation. Product lot has been returned to hospital pharmacy. Suspected adverse drug reaction (ADR) submitted to regulatory portal. Immediate quality investigation of Batch AMX-9903 requested.
